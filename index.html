Step 01 · The city

Where does your company need to be?

What I do

I map where your clients, suppliers, staff and visiting colleagues need to go: metro lines, the river corridor, the airport, the routes that decide your week. São Paulo is big; the right side of it saves hours.

You receive

  • A shortlist of 2–3 areas that fit your company

Step 03 · The property

Three paths, chosen from your plans.

What we do

Corporate floors, a lease or a purchase: each has its advantages, and everything starts from your plans and needs.

  • Corporate floorsFull office floors in hubs such as Faria Lima or Berrini, with building services and flexible layouts.
  • LeaseMove in quickly, with less capital tied up, while you get to know the city.
  • PurchaseOne address for several uses, a path to residence and no rent readjustments.

You receive

  • Visit notes and photos for each property
  • Document checklist and a fit-out estimate with technical partners

Step 1 of 5

Buy or rent

For a mid-sized company, buying often solves more than it costs.

Most foreign companies rent by reflex. Here is the comparison I go through with each client.

RentingBuying
UsesSeparate contracts for warehouse, shop, office and staff housing.One address for all of them, where zoning allows it.
ResidenceNo effect on immigration.Property above the legal minimum can support a residence permit for the investor.
ReadjustmentsContracts in force rose 4.47% in 12 months (FGV IVAR); asking rents 6.63% (FipeZap).No rent to renegotiate. Condo fees and IPTU remain.
AssetPayments leave the company.The property stays on the books, in the currency the company now earns in.
≠

But buying is not always the answer.

Corporate floors and short stays usually make more sense rented. With the Selic at 13.75% a year, money has a price: every recommendation I make comes with the numbers, including the ones that argue against buying.
