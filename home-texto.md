## Parágrafo de abertura

> Foreign companies rarely fail in Brazil for lack of a business case. They
> fail on the ground: a labour rule read too late, a meeting where everyone
> agreed and nothing moved, an executive whose family never settled.
>
> Live & Learn Brazil works on both layers. Our team brings legal,
> tax, administrative, linguistic and educational expertise to companies
> establishing themselves in Brazil — and to Brazilian companies operating
> with partners abroad, particularly in China.
>
> We do not deliver generic training or off-the-shelf reports. We collect
> data inside your organisation, analyse it against validated international
> research, and build the strategy together with your team.

> technical and operational teams — because each area has different ways of
> solving the problem. From that, we build actions together to resolve the
